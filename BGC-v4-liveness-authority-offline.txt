VAN DE (v3 con thieu): round-robin cung -> neu authority den luot OFFLINE thi
chuoi KET (khong ai thay the). v4 giai quyet bang co che kieu Clique.

CO CHE (bgc_engine.py):
  1. KHOI TRAI LUOT HOP LE: khoi o chi so i do authority[i%N] ky la "dung luot"
     (trong so 2). Neu nguoi do offline, authority KHAC co the ky "trai luot"
     (trong so 1) de chuoi khong ket.
  2. FORK-CHOICE THEO TONG TRONG SO (khong phai do dai): nhanh nhieu khoi "dung luot"
     se nang hon -> khi nguoi dung luot online lai, trat tu chuan tu dong thang.
  3. LUAT "KHONG KY LIEN TIEP" (recent-signer, LIMIT=N//2): 1 authority khong duoc
     ky 2 khoi lien tiep -> chong doc chiem khi thieu nguoi; can DA SO online.
  4. FINALITY (v3) van giu: khoi cu hon FINALITY khong bi reorg.

KET QUA CHAY (bgc_demo4.py - TAT CA PASS):
  - Auth1 offline: chuoi van cao len 10; Auth1 ky 0 khoi; Auth0 & Auth2 thay nhau
    ky (xen ke, khong lien tiep); 21tr giu nguyen.
  - Trong so: nhanh co Auth1 offline = 13; nhanh tat ca dung luot (Auth1 online) = 20
    -> nhanh dung luot nang hon -> fork-choice uu tien trat tu chuan khi du nguoi.
  - 3 NODE THAT: Auth1 khong ket noi; Auth0 & Auth2 van dao va dong bo (height 9,
    cung tip, 21tr). 1/3 offline KHONG lam ket mang.
