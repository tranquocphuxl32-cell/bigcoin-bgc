SU THAT: "dua vao thuc te" IT ve viet them code (da gan du), NHIEU ve TRIEN KHAI +
HE SINH THAI + CONG DONG - dung nhu BTC da di tu 2009. Duoi day map lich su BTC
sang hanh dong cu the cho BGC, kem thu DA CO trong thu muc nay.

--- GIAI DOAN 1: CONG KHAI (Satoshi 2008-2009) ---
B1. Whitepaper + MA NGUON MO len GitHub cong khai (giay phep MIT nhu Bitcoin).
    -> Da co: toan bo code trong Bigcoin-BGC + whitepaper + file dinh vi.
    -> Viec can lam: tao repo github.com/<ten>/bigcoin, day code len, README = thong diep.
B2. Genesis fair-launch (khong premine) + bits/target cong bo cong khai.
    -> Da co: bgcfl_engine (fair-launch PoW, halving). Can: dat tham so THAT truoc khi chot.

--- GIAI DOAN 2: MANG SONG (2009-2010) ---
B3. SEED NODES: 2-3 may chu VPS co IP tinh chay node 24/7 lam diem ket noi dau tien.
    (BTC dung "DNS seeds".) Nguoi moi tro vao seed de tim mang.
    -> Can: thue VPS (vai USD/thang), mo cong, chay run_node/bgcfl_net voi IP that.
    -> Da co: P2P ma hoa + reconnect; chi can dat len may chu that.
B4. DAO cong khai: bat ky ai tai code -> chay miner -> gop hashrate. Fair launch.
    -> Da co: bgcfl_net (nhieu tho dao). Can: run_miner CLI than thien (giong run_node).

--- GIAI DOAN 3: NGUOI DUNG DUNG DUOC (vi + explorer) ---
B5. VI cho nguoi khong ky thuat: tao dia chi, xem so du, gui/nhan. (Desktop truoc, mobile sau.)
    -> Da co: bgc_wallet (luu khoa an toan) + lenh bal/send trong run_node.
    -> Viec can lam: dong goi thanh app 1-click (vd PyInstaller) de nguoi thuong cai dat.
B6. BLOCK EXPLORER website: ai cung xem chuoi/so du qua URL -> minh bach + niem tin.
    -> DA CO & CHAY: bgc_explorer_server.py (xem anh bgc_explorer_preview.html).
    -> Viec can lam: chay tren VPS + ten mien (vd explorer.bigcoin.org).
B7. FAUCET + tai lieu: phat 1 it BGC mien phi cho nguoi moi thu gui/nhan; huong dan cai dat.

--- GIAI DOAN 4: CONG DONG & TIEN ICH (2010-2013) ---
B8. Cong dong: forum/Discord/Telegram + docs. Nguoi dam me chay node, dao, dong gop code.
B9. Tien ich THAT: dung escrow AI/robot (bgc_job) cho dich vu that -> gia tri tu SU DUNG.
B10. Dich vu/merchant chap nhan BGC; cau noi vi AI-agent voi dich vu AI that.

--- GIAI DOAN 5: THANH KHOAN & NIEM YET (sau, than trong) ---
B11. Khi da phi tap trung + co thanh khoan: xin niem yet san (san tu tham dinh phap ly).
     -> CAN luat su truoc. Luc dau nen Reg S/geoblock US (xem file phap ly da luu).

=== CHI PHI & HA TANG THUC TE (noi thang) ===
  - VPS seed/explorer: vai chuc USD/thang. Ten mien: ~10 USD/nam.
  - Thoi gian van hanh + ho tro cong dong: dang ke, lien tuc.
  - Bao mat: code hoc tap nay CHUA qua kiem toan doc lap -> TRUOC khi co gia tri that,
    phai thue audit + kiem thu tai cao. Dung de tien that trso vao code chua audit.
  - Phap ly: giu thong diep trung thuc (khong hua loi nhuan); hoi luat su truoc khi
    cham toi nguoi My; VN cung co quy dinh rieng ve tai san ao - nen tim hieu.

=== THU DA SAN SANG vs CAN LAM ===
  SAN: node, P2P ma hoa, fair-launch PoW (retarget/maturity/chainwork), vi khang luong tu,
       explorer web (chay that), escrow AI/robot, finality/liveness (ban PoA), huong dan lien may.
  CAN: dat tham so mainnet that + chot genesis; VPS seed nodes + ten mien; dong goi vi 1-click;
       run_miner CLI; faucet; cong dong + docs; AUDIT bao mat; luat su.
