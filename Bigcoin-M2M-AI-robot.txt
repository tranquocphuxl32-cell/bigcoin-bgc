  - Robot giao hang: lam viec vat ly, tu tra tien dien/sac (M2M).
  - Tram sac: ban dien cho robot, dong gop lai quy phat trien AI.
  => BGC la don vi thanh toan cho CA tri tue (AI) LAN lao dong vat ly (robot).

  4. Premine + PoA: van phai tin founder (giu 11tr, ky khoi). Xem whitepaper muc 6.
  5. May chi tieu TU DONG -> phai dat HAN MUC/quy tac chi tieu, keo robot loi hoac
     bi chiem se tieu sach quy. (Vd: robot chi duoc tra toi da X BGC/ngay.)
