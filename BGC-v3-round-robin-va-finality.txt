HAI NANG CAP de khong ai mot minh viet lai lich su:

1. ROUND-ROBIN PoA (nhieu authority luan phien) - bgc_engine.py
   - Khoi o chi so i BAT BUOC do authority[i % N] ky (N = so authority).
   - 1 authority KHONG the mot minh dao chuoi dai: can khoa cua CA NHOM de ky
     cac chi so xen ke -> giam doc quyen & kha nang kiem duyet cua 1 ben.
   - Node authority chi dao KHI DEN LUOT (Node.my_turn()).

2. FINALITY (chot do sau reorg) - bgc_engine.py (FINALITY=6)
   - Khoi cach tip >= FINALITY duoc COI NHU CHOT. Nhanh canh tranh fork SAU diem
     chot -> BI TU CHOI du dai hon va ky dung round-robin.
   - Chong "viet lai lich su": lich su cu khong the bi dao.
   - Reorg NONG (trong vung chua chot) van hoat dong binh thuong.

KET QUA CHAY (bgc_demo3.py - TAT CA PASS):
  - 3 node: A & B la authority luan phien, C chi QUAN SAT -> ca 3 HOI TU cung chuoi.
  - Doc chuoi da hoi tu: moi khoi do dung authority[i%2] ky, A&B xen ke.
  - Finality: fork do sau 2 (<6) -> reorg OK; fork SAU diem chot (>6, dai hon nhieu)
    -> BI TU CHOI (tip khong doi). 21tr giu nguyen suot.

LUU Y QUAN TRONG CHO ANH:
  1. Round-robin can them co che "bo luot" khi 1 authority offline (liveness) - chua co.
     Neu authority den luot ma chet, chuoi se ket cho toi khi co co che timeout/skip.
  2. Nhieu authority = tin cay PHAN TAN HON 1 nguoi, nhung VAN la "federation" (tap
     cho phep co dinh), CHUA phai phi tap trung mo nhu PoW/PoS cua BTC. Muon mo thuc su
     can doi co che dong thuan (vd PoS) - huong dai han.
  3. FINALITY=6 la vi du; chon theo nhu cau (cao hon = an toan hon truoc reorg, nhung
     cham "chung thuc cuoi cung" hon).
