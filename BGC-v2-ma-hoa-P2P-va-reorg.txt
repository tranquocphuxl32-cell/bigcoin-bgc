HAI NANG CAP de BGC san sang cho mang nhieu node khong tin nhau:

1. MA HOA P2P (bgc_secure.py) - kieu BIP324
   - Bat tay ECDH (secp256k1, khoa epheral moi ket noi -> forward secrecy).
   - HKDF-SHA256 dan xuat 2 khoa huong; ChaCha20-Poly1305 (RFC8439) ma hoa+xac thuc.
   - Moi ban tin P2P = 1 khung ma hoa [4 byte len][ciphertext+tag 16B].
   - Ke nghe len KHONG doc duoc (ma hoa) va KHONG sua duoc (MAC sai -> ngat).
   - LUU Y: send() co KHOA (lock) -> nhieu luong gui an toan (tranh dua nonce).

2. FORK-CHOICE / REORG (bgc_engine.py v2)
   - Kho khoi theo hash; chon NHANH DAI NHAT hop le lam 'active'; hoa thi giu nhanh
     cu (first-seen). Khi co nhanh dai hon -> tu REORG (tai tao UTXO tu genesis).
   - Tx o nhanh bi bo quay lai mempool; reorg KHONG tao/mat coin (21tr bat bien).
   - Dong bo dinh ky (ticker hello moi 0.3s) -> cac node HOI TU chac chan.

KET QUA CHAY (ca 2 demo PASS):

*** LUU Y QUAN TRONG ***
  1. Fork-choice o day = 'nhanh dai nhat' (PoA). That te can them: chong spam khoi,
     gioi han do sau reorg (finality), nhieu authority luan phien de khong ai doc quyen.
  2. Ma hoa KENH dung ECDH CO DIEN: neu lo luong tu chi lo SIEU DU LIEU mang (ai noi
     voi ai), KHONG lo tien (chu ky coin van hash-based PQ). Nang KEM hau luong tu
     (ML-KEM/Kyber) khi can.
  3. Day van la prototype hoc tap: can kiem thu tai cao, chong DoS, va danh gia bao mat
     doc lap truoc khi dung that.
