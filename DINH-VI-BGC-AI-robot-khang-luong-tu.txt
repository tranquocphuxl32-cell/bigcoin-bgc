       - BTC muon khang luong tu phai soft-fork trong tuong lai, rui ro+tranh cai;
         BGC co san tu khoi 0. Day la ly do ky thuat THAT de ton tai song song BTC.
   (B) LOP THANH TOAN GOC CHO MAY MOC (AI + ROBOT HINH NGUOI)
       - Moi AI-agent / robot = 1 vi tu tri, tu ky, tu tra tien cho nhau (M2M).

       - BTC khong co danh tinh may + escrow M2M gon san; BGC thiet ke cho dieu do.
