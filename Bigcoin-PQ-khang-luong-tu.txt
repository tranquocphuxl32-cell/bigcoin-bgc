LA CHAN: CHU KY DUA VAO HAM BAM (HASH-BASED)
  Ham bam SHA-256 la MOT CHIEU; may luong tu KHONG dao nguoc duoc no:
    - SHOR: chi pha duoc bai toan log roi rac (ECDSA/RSA) -> VO DUNG voi hash.
    - GROVER: chi tang toc tim tien anh x2 (256 bit -> 128 bit) -> van an toan.
  Nen neu chu ky CHI dua vao SHA-256, coin khang duoc luong tu. Day la huong
  NIST da chuan hoa (SP 800-208: XMSS/LMS; va SPHINCS+ khong trang thai).

CACH DUNG TRONG BGC-PQ (tu viet, chay that)
  1. WOTS+ (Winternitz One-Time Signature): chu ky MOT LAN, chi gom cac chuoi bam
     SHA-256. Khoa cong = dinh cac chuoi; chu ky = diem giua chuoi; nguoi kiem bam
     tiep len dinh roi so sanh. An toan = khong dao nguoc duoc chuoi bam.
  2. XMSS: 1 dia chi = GOC CAY MERKLE cua 2^h khoa WOTS (demo h=4 -> ky 16 lan,
     founder h=6 -> 64 lan). Ky lan thu k: dung khoa WOTS thu k + duong Merkle.
     Kiem: tai tao khoa WOTS tu chu ky -> bam thanh la -> di len goc -> so dia chi.
  => DIA CHI LA 1 HASH 32 BYTE. KHONG he co "khoa cong EC" nao lo ra bao gio.
